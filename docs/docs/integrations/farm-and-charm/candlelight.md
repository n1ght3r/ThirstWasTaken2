With Candlelight installed, its soups, salads and chocolate mousse restore thirst, and its kitchen
sinks give water that still needs boiling.

::: warning Supported versions
Fabric and NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qwbArkQk/5e0770c8da0fab82a70bc9c3913c8d3996c53345_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Let's Do: Candlelight](https://modrinth.com/mod/lets-do-candlelight-farmcharm-compat)
2.1.13, which needs [Farm & Charm](./) and Architectury API. Its 1.20.1 version is no longer updated and
is not supported.
:::

## Kitchen sinks

Candlelight's kitchen sinks fill from nothing, as they always have. The water they give, in a bottle
or a bucket, is Murky, so it still needs boiling. A sink refuses sea water.

![A player at a Candlelight kitchen, holding a bottle just filled at the sink, its tooltip reading Murky](/screenshots/integrations/farm-and-charm/candlelight-sink-murky-bottle.png)

## Cooking

The Cooking Pot takes a water bucket the same way Farm & Charm's does: any grade of fresh water works,
and sea water does not.

## Food

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup, Salad, Beetroot Salad, Fresh Garden Salad | 4 | 5 |
| Tomato Mozzarella Salad | 3 | 4 |
| Chocolate Mousse | 2 | 3 |

Pasta, roasts, steaks and the dishes cooked in wine restore no thirst. Every value can be changed in
the [config](/docs/configuration).
