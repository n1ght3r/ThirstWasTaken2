# ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Eh11TaTm/cea48ad39e9323e9e0f5354ee1d4c160f46b50be_96.webp){.mod-icon} Let's Do: HerbalBrews

With HerbalBrews installed, its teas and coffees restore thirst, also when drunk from a Jug, and the Tea
Kettle won't brew with sea water.

::: warning Supported versions
Fabric and NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Eh11TaTm/cea48ad39e9323e9e0f5354ee1d4c160f46b50be_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Let's Do: HerbalBrews](https://modrinth.com/mod/lets-do-herbalbrews)
1.1.4, which needs Architectury API. Its 1.20.1 version is no longer updated and is not supported.
:::

## Tea Kettle

The Tea Kettle takes a water bucket or a water bottle in its water slot. Any grade of fresh water works,
since the kettle boils it. Sea water fills nothing: the bucket or bottle stays in the slot, and no tea is
brewed.

![The Tea Kettle's screen with a bucket of sea water in its water slot, its tooltip reading Salty, and the water gauge empty](/screenshots/integrations/herbalbrews/herbalbrews-kettle-salty.png)

## Jug

A placed Jug holds up to three cups. Drinking from it counts every cup in it, so three teas from a Jug
restore as much as three teas drunk one by one.

## Drinks

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Green, Black, Oolong, Lavender, Yerba Mate, Rooibos and Hibiscus Tea, Coffee, Milk Coffee | 6 | 4 |

The Flask, a mix of three potions, restores no thirst. Every value can be changed in the
[config](/docs/configuration).
