With Croptopia installed, its drinks, soups and juicier fruit restore thirst, and sea water can't be
used in its recipes.

::: warning Supported versions
Fabric and NeoForge on Minecraft 26.2, 26.1 and 1.21.1, and Fabric and Forge on 1.20.1, with
![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/308/636/64/64/637392485303151332.png){.mod-icon} [Croptopia](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/croptopia)
4.3.2, 4.2.4 or 4.0.1, which needs EpheroLib. Croptopia is on CurseForge only. It has no version for
26.3 or 1.21.11.
:::

Croptopia's Water Bottle and Milk Bottle are ingredients and can't be drunk. Every value can be changed
in the [config](/docs/configuration).

## Water in recipes

Croptopia crafts sixteen Water Bottles from one water bucket, and its tea, steamed rice, soups and dough
take a Water Bottle or a water bucket in the crafting grid. A bucket of sea water won't work in any of
them.

Fresh water of any grade still works. A crafting recipe can't tell how clean the water was, so
Croptopia's Water Bottles have no grade, and the drinks made from them are safe.
