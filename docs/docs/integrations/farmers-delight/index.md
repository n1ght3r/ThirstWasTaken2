Clean, and Nourishment stops thirst draining. Nothing needs to be set up.

## Drinks and meals

![Farmer's Delight drinks and bone broth in the hotbar, with the bone broth tooltip showing thirst and hunger](/screenshots/integrations/farmers-delight/farmers-delight-drinks.png)

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Apple cider, melon juice, hot cocoa, bone broth, beef stew, chicken soup, vegetable soup, fish stew, pumpkin soup, baked cod stew, noodle soup, onion soup | 6 | 4 |
| Milk bottle | 4 | 0 |
| Tomato, melon popsicle, fruit salad, mixed salad | 2 | 0 |
| Pumpkin slice, cabbage leaf, glow berry custard, tomato sauce | 1 | 0 |

Every value can be changed in the [config](/docs/configuration#drinks-and-foods).

## Boiling water in the Cooking Pot

![A murky water bowl cooking in the Cooking Pot, with a pure water bowl ready to serve](/screenshots/integrations/farmers-delight/farmers-delight-cooking-pot.png)

A heated Cooking Pot boils a Dirty or Murky water bottle or terracotta water bowl Clean in ten
seconds. Clean and Pure water have nothing left to boil, so the pot doesn't take them.

- A bowl comes out ready to take.
- A bottle is served like the pot's own drinks: place a glass bottle in the container slot. On
  Minecraft 1.20.1 to 1.21.1 it comes out ready to take.
- Salt water is refused.

## Nourishment

While Nourishment is active, thirst does not drain. Salt water still costs thirst.
