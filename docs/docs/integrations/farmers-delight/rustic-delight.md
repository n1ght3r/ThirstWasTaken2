With Rustic Delight installed, its coffees, soups, sweet salad and bell peppers restore thirst.

::: warning Supported versions
Everything on this page works on Fabric on every supported Minecraft version, and on NeoForge 1.21.1,
with [Rustic Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/rustic-delight) 1.7. On Fabric it needs Farmer's Delight
Refabricated.
:::

## Drinks and foods

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Syrup, Chocolate, Honey, Pumpkin and Cherry Blossom Coffee | 8 | 11 |
| Milk Coffee | 6 | 10 |
| Coffee | 5 | 8 |
| Dark Coffee | 4 | 6 |
| Syrup | 2 | 3 |
| Bell Pepper Soup, Calamari Soup, Sweet Salad | 4 | 5 |
| Bell Pepper, every color | 2 | 3 |
| Bell Pepper Slice, every color | 1 | 2 |

Roasted, stuffed and rolled bell peppers, Potato Salad, Cooking Oil, Batter and the fried foods restore
none. Every value can be changed in the [config](/docs/configuration#drinks-and-foods).
