With Ocean's Delight installed, its Guardian Soup and two of its bowls restore thirst.

::: warning Supported versions
Everything on this page works on Fabric and NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with
![](https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.modrinth.com%2Fdata%2FDGiq4ZSW%2F949ba66d6fffb5a984fbb70e3ef4a51f15be3191.png&trim=1&w=96&h=96&fit=contain&cbg=00000000&output=png){.mod-icon .mod-icon-lg} [Ocean's Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/oceans-delight) 1.0.3 on Fabric and 1.0.4 on NeoForge. On
Fabric it needs Farmer's Delight Refabricated. The mod has no build for a newer Minecraft version.
:::

## Foods

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Bowl of Guardian Soup | 5 | 7 |
| Braised Sea Pickle | 4 | 5 |
| Seagrass Salad | 2 | 3 |

Squid rings, rolls, tentacles, cod and the other seafood restore none. Every value can be changed in the
[config](/docs/configuration#drinks-and-foods).
