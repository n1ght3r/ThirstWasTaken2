title: "Youkais' Feasts"
---

With Gensokyo Delight ~ Youkais' Feasts installed, its teas, juices, sakes, wines, soups and popsicles
restore thirst.

::: warning Supported versions
Everything on this page works on NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with
![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Ohy0TgO2/4e25b7b5de37ad03845970ec8ef72f5c0b8e4617_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Youkais' Feasts](https://modrinth.com/mod/gensokyo-delight-youkais-feasts) 1.1.2, and on Forge, Minecraft 1.20.1, with
0.5.2. The mod has no Fabric build and no build for a newer Minecraft version.
:::

## Drinks and foods

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Every tea, Green Water, Black, Red and White Grape Juice, every soup and stew | 6 | 4 |
| Mead, Mio | 5 | 2 |
| Every other sake, Red and White Wine, Burgundy, Champagne, Van Allen | 3 | 1 |
| Milk and Big Popsicle, Shaved Ice Over Rice, Shirayuki, Peach Tapioca, Tutu Congee, Cucumber | 2 | 0 |
| Cucumber Slice, Black, Red and White Grape | 1 | 0 |

Soy sauce, mayonnaise, sushi and other dry foods restore none. Every value can be changed in the
[config](/docs/configuration#drinks-and-foods).

## Water in its kitchen

- The Kettle, the Fermentation Tank and the iron pots take fresh water of any grade. Tea, sake and soup
  are safe whatever went into them.
- A bucket or bottle of sea water won't go into the Kettle or the Fermentation Tank, won't start a soup
  in an iron pot, and won't cook Bamboo Mizuyokan in the Cooking Pot. Anything made from it would come
  out safe, which would make the sea drinkable.
