# Extra Delight

With Extra Delight installed, its juices, ades, milkshakes, coffee, tea and soups restore thirst, and
its taps, jars, kegs and kitchen blocks treat water the way the rest of this mod does.

::: warning Supported versions
Everything on this page works on NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with

## Water in its blocks

- **Jars, Kegs, and the Vat, Mixing Bowl and Chiller** keep the grade of the water poured in, by bottle
  or by bucket. Dirty water comes back out Dirty, and sea water stays sea water. A tank holding one grade
  won't take another.
- **The Tap and the Sinks** still never run dry, but their water is Murky, the same as a kitchen sink from
  other mods.
- **A Funnel** taking up a water source gets that water's grade, so a funnel at the coast collects sea
  water.
- **Melted ice**, from the Chiller's drip tray or the Melting Pot, is Clean, like rain in a cauldron.
- **The Vat and the Mixing Bowl** make nothing from sea water. Fresh water of any grade works. Sea water
  crafts no Vinegar Pot or Yeast Pot either.

## Salt

The Evaporator only dries sea water, into salt. Fresh water left in it stays as it is. Its salt counts as
salt for the [Copper Distiller](/docs/features/water-purity#copper-distiller).
