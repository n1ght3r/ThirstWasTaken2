With Fruits Delight installed, its juices, teas, jellos, popsicles and juicy fruits restore thirst.

::: warning Supported versions
Everything on this page works on NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with
[Fruits Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/fruits-delight) 1.2.14. The mod has no Fabric build and no
build for a newer Minecraft version.
:::

Jam, cookies, pies and other dry foods restore none. Every value can be changed in the
[config](/docs/configuration#drinks-and-foods).

## Water in its recipes

- Juice made from a water bottle, and the lemonade and fruit cauldrons made from a full cauldron of
  water, take fresh water of any grade. The juice is safe whatever went into it, as tea is.
- A bottle of sea water makes no juice, and a cauldron of sea water takes no lemon slice or jam. Juice
  or jello from it would come out safe, which would make the sea drinkable.
