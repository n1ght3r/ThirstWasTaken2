With No Man's Land installed, its new swamps give Dirty water, the sea at its Mud Beach is salty, its
drinks and pears restore thirst, and sipping from its milk cauldron counts as drinking milk.

::: warning Supported versions
NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/kjZCvAn6/958489a1729e9e17a6a5a0728ef249236c07f7b3_96.webp){.mod-icon} [No Man's Land](https://modrinth.com/mod/no-mans-land)
1.5.12. It has no version for any other loader or Minecraft version, so they ignore it.
:::

![A bayou of dark water, cypress trees and lily pads in No Man's Land](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/kjZCvAn6/images/8a3c12e671bf62efe2875616f539c246e91dd31f.png)

## Water in its biomes

| Biome | Water |
|---|---|
| Bog, Bayou, Dark Swamp | Dirty, the same as a vanilla swamp |
| Blackwater River | Dirty: it is a slow swamp river, not a clear one |
| Mud Beach | Salty, the same as any other beach |
| Desert River, Lush River | Murky, the same as any other river |

![Looking at the water of a No Man's Land Bog, with Jade naming it Dirty](/screenshots/integrations/no-mans-land/bog-water.png)

How grades are worked out is on the [Water Purity](/docs/features/water-purity#the-four-grades) page.

## Milk cauldron

A milk bucket poured into a cauldron gives four sips from an empty hand. Each sip restores a quarter of
what the milk bucket restores, so a full cauldron is worth about one bucket.

![A cauldron of milk on the bank of a bog, a cow behind it, with Jade naming it Cauldron Filled with Milk](/screenshots/integrations/no-mans-land/milk-cauldron.png)

## Drinks and food

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Pear Juice | 8 | 13 |
| Maple Syrup Bottle | 4 | 6 |
| Pesto Bottle | 1 | 2 |
| Witch Stew | 4 | 5 |
| Pear, Syruped Pear, Honeyed Apple | 2 | 3 |

Pear Juice, the Pesto Bottle and Witch Stew only exist with Farmer's Delight installed. Meat, fish, nuts,
bread, pasta and cakes restore no thirst. Every value can be changed in the [config](/docs/configuration).
