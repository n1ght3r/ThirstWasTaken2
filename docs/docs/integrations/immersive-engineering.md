# ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/tIm2nV03/icon.png){.mod-icon} Immersive Engineering

With ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/tIm2nV03/icon.png){.mod-icon} [Immersive Engineering](https://modrinth.com/mod/immersiveengineering) installed, water keeps its
grade in its pumps, pipes, barrels and bottles.

::: warning Supported versions
NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1, with Immersive Engineering 12.4.2, and Forge on Minecraft 1.20.1, with
Immersive Engineering 10.2.0. Immersive Engineering has no build for any other loader or Minecraft
version, so they ignore it.
:::

## Fluid Pump

The Fluid Pump takes the grade of the water it pumps, the same as a bucket would. A pump in the sea
fills up with sea water, and one in a swamp with Dirty water.

A pump holding one grade waits until its tank is empty before it takes water of another grade, so two
grades never mix in its tank.

## Bottling Machine

A glass bottle filled in the Bottling Machine gets the grade of the water in the machine. Sea water
makes bottles of sea water.

## Pipes, barrels and the Jerrycan

Water keeps its grade through Fluid Pipes, the Wooden and Metal Barrel, the Fluid Tank and the
Jerrycan. Water of different grades stays apart: a Jerrycan only tops up with water of the grade it
already holds.

How grades are worked out is on the [Water Purity](/docs/features/water-purity#the-four-grades) page.
