With Bountiful Fares installed, its Cups hold graded water like a glass bottle, its teas, ciders and
stews restore thirst, and sea water can't be fermented or brewed into tea.

::: warning Supported versions
Fabric and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1, with
![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/YgrSDjlb/f4e10611dcc1791138225224fa444f5a8ef4348f_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Bountiful Fares](https://modrinth.com/mod/bountiful-fares)
3.0.13, which needs NexusLib. Its older versions for 1.20 are no longer updated and are not supported.
:::

## The Cup

A Cup works like a glass bottle. Filled from water, it gets that water's
[grade](/docs/features/water-purity), so a Cup filled from the sea holds sea water. A Water Cup is
drunk like a water bottle and restores the same thirst, with the same chance of sickness from dirty
water.

Water Cups pour into and draw from cauldrons, the Hanging Pot and the Copper Distiller one drink at a
time, and the water keeps its grade.

## Fermentation and tea

The Fermentation Vessel won't take sea water. Fresh water of any grade still works, and what comes out
is safe to drink.

Tea crafted from a Water Cup of sea water won't work, and neither will a Sponge Cake made with buckets
of sea water.
