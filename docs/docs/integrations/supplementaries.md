# Supplementaries

With Supplementaries installed, water keeps its grade in Jars, Goblets and Faucets, and a Jar or a
Goblet of water can be drunk.

::: warning Supported versions
Fabric and NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1: ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/fFEIiSDQ/e9f5f66fa3b67e54acb91258a1428d68311c58bc_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Supplementaries](https://modrinth.com/mod/supplementaries)
1.21.1-3.9.9. Other Minecraft versions ignore Supplementaries, which has no build for them.
:::

![Five jars of water on a lakeshore, brown, grey, blue, cyan and turquoise, with Jade naming the middle one Clean](/screenshots/integrations/supplementaries/supplementaries-jars.png)

## Water grades

- Water poured into a Jar or a Goblet keeps its grade, and a bottle filled from one gets that grade
  back. Without the mod every Jar handed out Clean water whatever went in.
- Sea water stays sea water. It cannot be drunk or boiled into anything else.
- Water of two grades does not mix. A Jar of Murky water refuses Clean water until it is empty.

## Seeing the grade

- The water in a Jar or a Goblet is coloured by its grade, from brown for Dirty to turquoise for sea
  water.
- A Jar broken while it holds water names the grade on its tooltip.
- With Jade installed, looking at a Jar or a Goblet shows the grade under the crosshair.

## Drinking

Right click a Jar or a Goblet of water with an empty hand to drink one serving. It restores what the
same water in a bottle would, and bad water makes the drinker ill just the same.

Supplementaries' own settings still decide whether a Jar or a Goblet can be used this way.

## Faucets

![A Faucet pouring from a cauldron into a Copper Hanging Pot, with Jade naming the pot's water Murky](/screenshots/integrations/supplementaries/supplementaries-faucet.png)

- A Faucet keeps the grade of the water it moves, into and out of a cauldron. A cauldron poured into
  keeps the worse of the two grades, the same as pouring a container in by hand.
- A Faucet drawing from a lake, a river or a pool grades that water where it lies, the way filling a
  bottle there does.
- A Faucet fills and empties Copper and Iron Hanging Pots. A pot on a lit campfire goes on boiling
  while it fills.

## Terracotta bowls

A terracotta bowl fills and empties in a Jar, a Goblet or a Faucet the way a glass bottle does, and
holds one serving.
