# ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cuIIkdlx/748a556a3658f0a5f67068f4d8d5cf0041b794a7.png){.mod-icon} Kaleidoscope Chinese Food

With Kaleidoscope Chinese Food installed, its teas, soups, porridges and noodles restore thirst.

::: warning Supported versions
NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, and Forge, Minecraft 1.20.1, with ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cuIIkdlx/748a556a3658f0a5f67068f4d8d5cf0041b794a7.png){.mod-icon}
[Kaleidoscope Chinese Food](https://modrinth.com/mod/kaleidoscopechinesefood) 1.1.14, which needs
[Kaleidoscope Cookery](./).
:::

## Teas, soups and noodles

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Yunnan Dianhong, Hong Kong Milk Tea, Fermented Bean Juice, Seaweed Egg Drop Soup, Tomato Egg Drop Soup, Century Egg & Pork Porridge, Pumpkin Porridge, Flatbread in Lamb Soup, Sichuan Spicy Pot | 6 | 4 |
| Wonton Noodles, Noodles with Beef and Pickled Vegetables, Spicy Sichuan Wonton | 3 | 2 |

They restore what Kaleidoscope Cookery's teas, soups and noodles do. Like every tea, the two teas are
brewed in the Teapot, so a cup is safe whatever water went in, and the Teapot brews neither from sea
water. The feasts eaten off a placed block, such as the Yellow Croaker Soup and the Sichuan
Fish Pot, are solid food and restore no thirst, as are the rice and wok dishes. Every value can be
changed in the [config](/docs/configuration#drinks-and-foods).
