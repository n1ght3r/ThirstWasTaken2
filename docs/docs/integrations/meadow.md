# ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ra7o6Sl7/d7e6f6bcaa82b9af4a670e6dd6cb980c970aa0f2_96.webp){.mod-icon} Let's Do: Meadow

With Meadow installed, its wooden bucket, wooden cauldron and Cooking Pot follow the same water rules
as everything else, and its wooden milk buckets restore thirst.

::: warning Supported versions
Fabric and NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1, with ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ra7o6Sl7/d7e6f6bcaa82b9af4a670e6dd6cb980c970aa0f2_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Let's Do: Meadow](https://modrinth.com/mod/lets-do-meadow)
1.4.10, which needs Architectury API. Its 1.20.1 version is no longer updated and is not supported.
:::

## Wooden bucket

A wooden bucket filled from the world carries the grade of the water it scooped, like an iron bucket.
Sea water stays sea water. The grade goes with it into a cauldron, a Hanging Pot or a Copper Distiller,
and a wooden bucket filled from those comes back with their grade.

## Wooden cauldron

Meadow's wooden cauldron is an open tub, so whatever is drawn from it is Murky, whether it was filled
by rain or with clean water. Boil it before drinking. It won't take sea water.

## Cooking Pot

Sea water won't go into the Cooking Pot. Any fresh water still cooks, since what comes out is food.

## Drinks

| Item | Thirst | Quenched |
|---|---|---|
| Every wooden milk bucket | 4 | 0 |

Cheese, sandwiches and buffalo meat are solid food and restore no thirst. Every value can be changed
in the [config](/docs/configuration).
