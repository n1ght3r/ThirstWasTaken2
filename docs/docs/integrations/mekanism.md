# ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Ce6I4WUE/ea185eb1300a64867f89101d4798e71a54ef6bed_96.webp){.mod-icon} Mekanism

With ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Ce6I4WUE/ea185eb1300a64867f89101d4798e71a54ef6bed_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Mekanism](https://modrinth.com/mod/mekanism) installed, water keeps its

water.

::: warning Supported versions
NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1, with Mekanism 10.7.19, and Forge on Minecraft 1.20.1, with Mekanism
10.4.16. Mekanism has no build for any other loader or Minecraft version, so they ignore it.
:::

## Electric Pump

The Electric Pump takes the grade of the water it pumps, the same as a bucket would. A pump in the sea
fills up with sea water, and one in a swamp with Dirty water.

A pump holding one grade leaves water of another grade where it is, so two grades never mix in its
tank.

## Rotary Condensentrator

Water condensed from water vapour comes out Pure, whatever it was before. Turning sea water into vapour
and back is a way to make it drinkable, at the cost of power.

## Pipes and tanks

Water keeps its grade through Mechanical Pipes, Fluid Tanks, the Dynamic Tank and the Portable Tank.
Water of different grades stays apart.

How grades are worked out is on the [Water Purity](/docs/features/water-purity#the-four-grades) page.
